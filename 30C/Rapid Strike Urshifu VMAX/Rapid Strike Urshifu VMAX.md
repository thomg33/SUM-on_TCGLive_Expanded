# Rapid Strike Urshifu VMAX

Author: Me

Source: Me

Date: 28 Oct 2026

Description: This deck can go first or second. If first, get out Urshifu, Remoraid, and as many Sobbles as possible. If second, get a Sobble to Active and use its attack to fill your bench. Use a combo of Shady Dealings, Irida, and Rapid Strike Search to evolve your Pokémon. Use Inteleon for chip damage and Urshifu for big damage (first attack) or very disruptive spread damage (second attack). Diancie Prism Star and Premium Power Pro add even more damage (both add damage to Urshifu’s second attack if you choose to hit the Active Spot).
